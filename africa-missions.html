Africa Missions & Evangelism Caravan (AMEC)

Mobilizing evangelists across Africa for mega missions, church planting, and the transformation of communities through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Program Overview

The Africa Missions & Evangelism Caravan (AMEC) is a mission program designed to mobilize evangelists from different churches and dioceses to participate in large-scale evangelistic missions across Africa.

Through these mega missions, believers are equipped and encouraged to embrace evangelism as a vital part of their Christian walk, bringing the message of Christ to communities in need.

AMEC has been instrumental in organizing impactful missions that lead to salvations, revival in churches, and the planting of new congregations across the continent.

AMEC Mission Activities

Door-to-Door Evangelism

Mission teams visit homes and businesses sharing the message of salvation and hope with individuals and families.

Open Air & Jesus Film

Large-scale evangelistic gatherings including gospel preaching and screenings of the Jesus Film.

Revival Meetings

Meetings that strengthen believers and inspire them to actively participate in evangelism.

School Outreach

Sharing the Gospel with students and equipping them with a strong spiritual foundation.

Prison Ministry

Reaching prisoners with messages of hope, forgiveness and redemption.

Church Planting

Establishing new churches where people can worship, be discipled and grow in faith.
