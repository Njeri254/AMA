Women’s Programme

Empowering women in mission, discipleship, and community transformation.

About the Program

The Women’s Programme seeks to mobilize, equip, and empower women to actively participate in mission and evangelism across Africa. Women play a critical role in spiritual formation, family transformation, and community development.

Key Focus Areas

Evangelism

Women actively participate in outreach and sharing the Gospel.

Discipleship

Strengthening faith through mentorship, teaching, and fellowship.

Leadership

Developing women into effective leaders within the church and community through various trainings and capacity building initiatives.

Community Impact

Transforming families and communities through Christian values. By offering child protection services and raising awareness about GBV (Gender-Based Violence), its causes, consequences, and prevention mechanisms.
