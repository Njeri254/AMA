Resource Mobilization

Supporting newly planted churches with the resources they need to grow, serve their communities, and advance the Gospel.

Strengthening Churches Through Resources

Anglican Missions Africa supports the growth of newly planted churches by mobilizing resources that enable ministry and outreach. Many young congregations face challenges such as lack of worship spaces, ministry equipment, and essential materials.

Through partnerships with donors and dioceses across Africa, we provide resources that help churches grow and effectively serve their communities.

💧 Drilling Hope Initiative

Drilling Hope: Quenching the Thirst of Communities in Need

At Anglican Missions Africa, our calling is not only to share the Gospel but also to respond to the practical needs of the communities we serve. One of the greatest challenges across many regions is access to clean and reliable water.

Families walk long distances, schools struggle with hygiene, and churches face limitations in outreach. We are trusting God for provision of a borehole drilling machine to serve these communities.

Reach remote communities with both spiritual and physical support

Increase long-term sustainability of the ministry

Provide clean, life-changing water to families, schools, and churches

Target: $100,000 USD
