Media & Digital Evangelism

Empowering the Church to communicate the Gospel effectively in the digital age.

Transforming Mission Through Media

Anglican Missions Africa is committed to equipping churches and ministry leaders with modern communication skills to effectively engage today’s digital world.

Through Media & Digital Evangelism, we train clergy, youth, and ministry teams in storytelling, content creation, and digital outreach strategies that amplify the message of Christ across online platforms.

Embracing the Digital Culture

As digital culture continues to redefine how people connect, communicate, and form communities, the Church is presented with both a challenge and an opportunity. Anglican Missions Africa (AMA) is responding boldly—by raising a new generation of digital missionaries equipped to proclaim the Gospel with creativity, excellence, and purpose across Africa’s fast-evolving digital spaces.

Media Is a Mission Field

Today, faith conversations are happening online as much as they are in physical spaces. Social media platforms, digital storytelling, and multimedia content have become powerful tools for influence and transformation. At AMA, media is not viewed merely as a communication tool; it is recognized as a vital mission field.

Our Media & Digital Evangelism Program

Through its Media and Digital Evangelism Program, AMA is pioneering innovative approaches that merge faith, media, and youth empowerment. The goal is simple yet profound: to ensure the Gospel remains present, visible, and impactful in the digital age.

Why This Program Matters

Our Media & Digital Evangelism Program responds to key needs within the Church and society; equipping believers, empowering youth, and transforming communities through impactful storytelling.

Church Communication

Equipping ministries with tools to share the Gospel clearly, creatively, and with excellence.

Youth Empowerment

Helping young people discover purpose, positive influence, and mission-driven identity.

Community Impact

Producing storytelling that uplifts, informs, and transforms communities.

Real Impact: Mombasa Media Training

A transformative one-week Media & Digital Evangelism training was conducted in ACK Mombasa Diocese, bringing together clergy, media teams, and youth leaders.

Participants explored modern communication trends, digital storytelling, and practical media production skills for ministry.

A highlight of the training included a hands-on visit to TV studios, where participants experienced real-world media environments and production workflows.
