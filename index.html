Statement of Faith

God is the Creator of the universe and that beside Him there is none.

The Bible is the inspired Word of God through which God’s divine art of creation is revealed.

Humanity sinned against God and therefore in constant need of God’s saving grace.

Jesus Christ is the only way, life, and truth through whom all humanity must be saved to eternal life.

The Church is the Christ-founded movement through which the gospel of salvation must be proclaimed to all humanity.

Church leadership has a divine mandate and responsibility to make disciples of all nations.

Every believer must come under Church discipline, restraining from worldly pleasure and sinful acts.
