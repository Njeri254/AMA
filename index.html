What We Do

Training

We equip church leaders and evangelists with the skills and knowledge needed to serve effectively through our accelerated church growth courses: Healthy Church and Capacity Building Program, Urban Orientation, Women’s Evangelism & Discipleship Program, Digital Evangelism and Church Music Renewal Programs These trainings provide practical tools and strategies that promote church growth, vitality, and community transformation. To date, we have trained over 17,000 leaders across Africa, nurturing strong and dynamic church communities on the continent.

Church Planting

One of our key activities is church planting, which involves establishing new churches in areas where the Anglican presence is limited or non-existent. We believe that by planting churches, we can create spaces of worship, community, and spiritual growth for individuals seeking solace and guidance. We are proud to announce that, we have successfully planted approximately 1000+ new churches since 2021 in the African continent and beyond.

Resource Mobilization

We understand the challenges faced by new churches in their early stages. To aid their growth and development, we actively engage in supporting these newly established churches. As part of our efforts, 130 tents have been distributed accross Africa, and 60+ churches supported with roofing ironsheets.
