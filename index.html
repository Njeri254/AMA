Trauma Healing Global Practice Conference

Anglican Missions Africa (AMA) participated in the Trauma Healing Global Practice Conference, a transformative two-and-a-half-day gathering organized by the Trauma Healing Institute in collaboration with Bible Societies across the world. Held at Pamevek Hotel, Nairobi – Westlands, the conference brought together practitioners dedicated to supporting communities affected by trauma. Our Director of Training and Capacity Building, Rev. Patience S. Wanzala, represented AMA and engaged in sessions centered on learning, relearning, reflection, and re-energizing for ministry. In Turkana County, AMA continues its work among vulnerable young men and women, journeying with them toward healing and restored hope. This conference strengthened our tools, perspectives, and partnerships for ongoing trauma-responsive ministry.

Clergy Healthy Church Training – Maseno North Diocese

Growing the Church Through Discipleship AMA concluded a three-day Healthy Church Training for Clergy at St. Philips in the ACK Maseno North Diocese. The training explored the dynamics of Church growth through discipleship, equipping approximately 70 clergy with practical tools for leading thriving and mission-oriented congregations. We extend heartfelt appreciation to Bishop Charles for hosting and supporting the initiative. Together, we are committed to building healthy, multiplying churches across Kenya.
