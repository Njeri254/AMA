School Outreach & Discipleship Ministry

Empowering teachers, chaplains, and volunteers to disciple students, strengthen Christian values, and foster spiritual growth in learning environments.

Equipping for Christ-Centered Education

School Outreach & Discipleship Ministry equips teachers, chaplains, and ministry partners with tools and spiritual grounding to disciple young people and integrate biblical values within educational settings.

Through training, mentorship, and practical engagement, this ministry seeks to nurture spiritually mature students and build communities that reflect the love of Christ.

Investing in the Next Generation of Leaders

At Anglican Missions Africa, we believe in investing in the next generation of leaders through our School Outreach Ministry program. With a focus on turning learning institutions into mission frontiers, we seek to reach thousands of young people with the message of hope found in Jesus Christ.

Through collaboration with established Christian unions and fellowships like Scripture Unions, Kenya Students Christian Fellowship (KSCF), Kenya Youth For Christ (KYFC), and Fellowship of Christian Union (FOCUS) Kenya, we strive to create an environment where young people can thrive spiritually.

Our School Outreach Ministry aims to counteract negative influences and secular trends by providing wholesome alternatives and promoting biblical values. Through events like our Wholesome Teen Tours and Chaplaincy Conferences, we offer opportunities for engagement, connection, and growth. In addition, our Learning Institutions Discipleship initiative provides primary through university students with access to valuable discipleship resources and support.

Join us today in supporting the development of tomorrow’s leaders through our School Outreach Ministry program. Together, we can make a difference in the lives of countless young people and ensure a bright future for generations to come.
